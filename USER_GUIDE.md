# Quick Start for Users

**Your Duty Roster PDF Generator in 5 minutes**

## What You Need

✅ A smartphone or computer with internet  
✅ A photo of the handwritten roster (clear, straight-on)  
✅ A reference PDF (blank template)  

That's it! No software to install.

## Step-by-Step

### 1. Open the App

Click the link you received: `https://duty-roster-generator.streamlit.app`

*Or navigate to it in your browser*

### 2. Choose Template Type

Select which roster format you're using:
- **"Nursing Officer"** — If it's a nursing staff roster
- **"Ward Boy / Aya"** — If it's a ward support staff roster
- **"Auto Detect"** — Let the app figure it out automatically

### 3. Upload Reference PDF

This is the blank/template PDF that you normally use.

- Click **"Upload PDF"** button
- Select your reference roster PDF
- Wait for it to upload (shows "100%")

### 4. Upload Your Handwritten Photo

This is the photo of the FILLED-IN handwritten roster.

**Tips for best results:**
- Take the photo straight-on (not at an angle)
- Ensure good lighting (avoid shadows)
- Hold camera steady and parallel to the page
- Make sure the entire roster fits in the frame
- Remove any glare or reflections

Click **"Upload Image"** and select your photo.

### 5. Extract the Data

Click the blue **"🔍 Extract Roster Data"** button.

The app will:
- ✅ Read your handwriting using AI
- ✅ Extract all names, positions, and duties
- ✅ Organize it into a table
- ✅ Flag any unclear handwriting (so you can fix it)

This takes about 10-20 seconds. You'll see a progress bar.

### 6. Review the Preview

Once extracted, you'll see:
- **Staff Table** — All extracted data in a table
- **Details** — Month, year, number of days, staff count

**Check if everything looks correct:**
- Scroll through the table
- Look for any red ⚠️ warnings about unclear entries
- If something is wrong, start over with a clearer photo

### 7. Generate the PDF

Click **"✨ Generate PDF"** to create your final output.

The app will:
- ✅ Fill your reference PDF with the new data
- ✅ Keep all original formatting
- ✅ Preserve signatures/important areas
- ✅ Create a clean single-page PDF

### 8. Download

Click **"⬇️ Download PDF"** to save the file.

The PDF will be named like: `roster_12_2024.pdf`

Your file is ready to print or share!

## Troubleshooting

### "Handwriting not read correctly"
- Take a clearer photo (straight, well-lit)
- Ensure handwriting is dark and legible
- Try again with better lighting
- The AI does its best, but clear writing helps!

### "Template not detected"
- Manually select your template type instead of "Auto Detect"
- Click "Extract" again

### "Upload failed"
- Check internet connection
- Try a smaller image file
- Refresh browser (F5) and try again

### "Something took too long"
- It sometimes takes 15-20 seconds
- Wait a moment and refresh
- If it keeps timing out, check your internet

## Tips for Best Results

📷 **Photo Quality**
- Natural light (no flash in dark room)
- Straight angle (not tilted)
- Entire roster visible
- No partial cutoffs

✏️ **Handwriting Quality**
- Use dark pen/pencil
- Write clearly and legibly
- Standard abbreviations (M, E, N, D/O, etc.)
- Don't overlap with printed text

📋 **Template Selection**
- Choose correct template type first
- If unsure, choose "Auto Detect"
- Your reference PDF should be the standard blank form

## What Information Is Extracted

The app reads:
- Hospital name (if present)
- Month and year
- Number of days
- Staff names
- Staff positions
- Duty codes for each day (M, E, N, D/O, L, etc.)
